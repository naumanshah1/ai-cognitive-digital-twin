# Behavioural Dataset — Data Dictionary

## Dataset Version & Metadata
- **Schema Version:** `0.1.0`
- **Initial Source:** Synthetic Behavioural Simulation (`app.services.synthetic_data`)
- **Record Granularity:** One user-day per row
- **Temporal Granularity:** Daily
- **Default Random Seed:** `42`
- **Intended Use:** Data pipeline verification, leakage auditing, baseline benchmarking, and controlled synthetic experiments.
- **Academic Context:** Nagarjuna College of Engineering and Technology, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

## 3. Target Specification and Leakage Prevention Policy

### 3.1 Synthetic Target Nature
`productivity_score` is a **synthetic heuristic outcome** constructed via the deterministic equation:
$$\text{Latent} = 30.0 + 4.0 \cdot \text{focus} + 3.0 \cdot (\text{sleep} - 7.0) + 3.0 \cdot \text{consistency} - 1.5 \cdot \text{interruptions} - 2.0 \cdot (\text{difficulty} - 3.0) + \mathcal{N}(0, 7.0)$$
$$\text{productivity\_score} = \text{clip}(\text{Latent}, 0.0, 100.0)$$

> **CRITICAL SCIENTIFIC CAVEAT:**
> Successful model convergence or high $R^2$ on this synthetic dataset demonstrates that the machine-learning pipeline can reconstruct this specific mathematical rule. It does **not** prove predictive accuracy on real human productivity. Empirical claims will only be made when evaluated on real-world datasets in subsequent research phases.

### 3.2 Leakage Rules
1. **Target Exclusion:** `productivity_score` is strictly excluded from feature matrix $X$.
2. **Target-Derived Exclusion:** `rolling_productivity_mean_7d` and `productivity_change_7d` are computed for exploratory longitudinal analysis but are programmatically forbidden from being included in predictor matrices for predicting productivity.
3. **Identifier & Timestamp Exclusion:** `user_id` and `record_date` are preserved in metadata partitions and never encoded as numerical predictors.
4. **Temporal Directionality:** All rolling statistics compute aggregates on `shift(1)`, guaranteeing that the current day's behavior does not contaminate historical metrics.
5. **Preprocessing Leakage:** Missing value imputers, outlier bounds, and scaling transformations are fitted **exclusively on the chronological training split** and applied freeze-transformed to validation and test splits.
